Frequently asked questions
Is it really free?
Yes. The app is free forever — no trial, no credit card, no catch. You do pay the AI company (like OpenAI) separately for the conversations you have, but that's usually $1–3 a month for normal use. We don't see that money.
What's an API key? Do I need to be technical?
An API key is just a password that lets Plictor connect to an AI service on your behalf. You get it by signing up at OpenAI.com — it takes about 2 minutes. No technical knowledge needed. If you can log into a website, you can do this.
Is my information private?
Plictor never sees your conversations. Your messages go directly from your computer to the AI service you chose (e.g. OpenAI). We have no server in the middle. We don't collect, store, or sell your data.
What AI services does it work with?
OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), Mistral, Groq, and more — 12 providers in total. You can switch between them anytime. If you just want to get started fast, OpenAI is the easiest.
What's the difference between Free and Pro?
Free gives you everything in the app — chat, search, tasks, voice. Pro adds persistent memory (Plictor remembers your preferences across sessions), a security suite, and scheduled background tasks. If you're not sure, start free and upgrade anytime.
Does it work on my computer?
Yes — Plictor Works runs on Windows 10/11, macOS 11+, and Linux. Download the version for your computer above.
